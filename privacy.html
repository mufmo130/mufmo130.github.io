Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy covers the tools and connectors published by Haidong Nan under the Apify username lsso (the "Services"), including Google Trends Scraper Pro, YouTube Transcript Extractor, App Review Insights and AI Search Visibility Tracker, whether used on Apify, through the Apify API, or through an AI assistant connector (MCP).

What we process

  • Your request: the inputs you or your AI assistant send, such as keywords, countries, time ranges, video or app links. They are used only to run your request and return the result.
  • Results: the output is stored in your own Apify account (datasets and key-value stores) under Apify's retention rules. We do not keep a separate copy.
  • Account and billing data: sign-in and payments are handled by Apify (and its payment processor). We do not receive your password or card details. We can see basic usage statistics that Apify provides to developers, such as the number of runs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not ask for personal data in inputs. App Review Insights does not collect reviewer names.
  • We do not use your inputs or results to train AI models.

Third parties

The Services read publicly available data from the websites they are built for (for example Google Trends, YouTube, the App Store and Google Play) and run on Apify's infrastructure. If you choose to provide your own API keys for AI providers, requests are sent to those providers under their terms. See Apify's privacy policy for how Apify processes account data.

Your choices

You can delete run data at any time from your Apify account. For questions or deletion requests, email mufmo130@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page when the Services change and show the new date above.
